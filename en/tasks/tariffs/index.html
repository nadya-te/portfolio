Payment flow redesign that grew average check by 30%

2024

Task

The payment flow no longer reflected the real product: new courses weren't showing on the pricing page, and users couldn't see the value of bundle offers. Incremental fixes weren't working — the business-critical flow needed a redesign strategy.

Role

  • Initiated a design discovery instead of continuing incremental fixes
  • Built cross-team collaboration: adjacent teams, CRM marketing, support
  • Broke the redesign into experiments to avoid risking metrics during peak season
  • Timed the launch for spring traffic to validate hypotheses before the peak

Result

  • +30% average check after the autumn launch
  • +10% in the spring test — confirmed the direction
